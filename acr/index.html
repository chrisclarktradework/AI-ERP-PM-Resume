90-day Plan

What the ninety days produce

01 · DAYS 1-14

Baseline what is actually there

Stakeholder interviews across Customer Service, Finance and IT. Map the sales order intake and the AR credits process as they genuinely run today rather than as documented. Establish the KPI baseline, because without it nothing that follows can be proved.

02 · DAYS 15-28

Rank the roadmap against the KPIs

Every candidate use case scored on value, feasibility and the data it depends on. The output is a sequence with owners and expected KPI movement, so the board sees a plan rather than a wish list.

03 · DAYS 29-42

Settle build versus buy

A structured evaluation of extending the existing vendor tooling against selecting a standalone automation platform, weighted against the S2K estate and run cost over three years. I ran this sequence at a $1B manufacturer, from stakeholder interviews through a competitive RFP to a selected partner, cutting roughly 22 percent from initial vendor bids.

04 · DAYS 43-56

Stand up governance that scales

A right-sized charter aligned to NIST AI RMF. Use-case intake, model registry, approval path and a human owning every decision. Sized for ACR today, built to hold as the estate grows through acquisition.

05 · DAYS 57-77

Put one automation into production

Not a pilot. Sales order intake or AR credits, whichever the roadmap ranks first, delivered on Azure DevOps in your three-week iteration rhythm with the KPI measured before and after.

06 · DAYS 78-90

Hand it over and leave

The operating rhythm transfers to the PMO and to your team. Steering cadence, intake process, reporting pack and the control tower. The engagement is designed to end, which is the difference between a consultant and a dependency.
